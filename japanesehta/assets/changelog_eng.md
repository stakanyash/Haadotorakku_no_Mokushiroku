# Changelog of all versions of Haadotorakku no Mokushiroku

### 1.0.3 [build 241212d]

- All voiceovers have been normalized by volume
- Removed the duplicate message about items left in your cabin or trunk when purchasing a new car
- The Oracle's phrase in Vatherland has been split because the text did not fit on the screen
- Special characters that appeared due to file encoding issues have been removed from quest descriptions
- The phrase of Lisa in Ridzin about the Crimson Dawn has been re-voiced. Previously, the name Crimson Dawn was incorrectly pronounced in the voiceover
- The main character's phrase before the tunnel in Librium has been re-voiced. Previously, there was a long pause in the word "bartender" in the voiceover
- In the cutscene after the battle with Felix at his base, the exclamation mark "!" in the line "So you're from Glukhoe" has been replaced with a question mark
- In the cutscene of the first arrival in Librium, an extra question mark has been removed from the stranger's line
- In the cutscene after the battle with Felix at his base, the capitalization of letters at the beginning of sentences in the main character's line about Lisa has been corrected
- Added a check for 100% game completion. Taken from [Community Disaster](https://github.com/zatinu322/stream_builds)
- Added a remastered version of the track from the credits, which will play upon 100% game completion

### 1.0.2 [build 241005a]

- Updated narrator voiceover
- Fixed incorrect translation in dialogue with Bukhara's
- The remaining "2tsu" have been replaced with the more correct "nitsu"
- The remaining "watashinochichi" have been replaced with the more correct "watashi no chichi"
- One of Lisa's lines in Arzhan is split into two different lines because the text didn't fit on the screen.

**[Download 1.0.2 version](https://github.com/stakanyash/Haadotorakku_no_Mokushiroku/releases/tag/1.0.2)**

### 1.0.1 [build 240922a]

- Fixed text in the dying father's cutscene where the main character's text did not fit on the screen
- "Watashinochichi" in dialogues has been corrected to the more correct "Watashi no chichi"
- Empty characters unsupported by Windows-1251 encoding have been removed from quest descriptions, which could cause two inappropriate question marks in the description
- In the dialogue with the old woman in Locus, the name of the city Midgard is now written with a capital letter
- In dialogues the name of the city of Porto is now written with a capital letter
- Fixed incorrect translation of the main character's line at the beginning of the cutscene of the first arrival at Ivan Go's house. In it, the main character said "pogoromu"
- In the dialogue with the Pesht guard, "2tsu" has been replaced with the more correct "nitsu" (the number "two" in Japanese romaji is written as "ni")

**[Download 1.0.1 version](https://github.com/stakanyash/Haadotorakku_no_Mokushiroku/releases/tag/1.0.1)**
